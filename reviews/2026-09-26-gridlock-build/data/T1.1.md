## Deviations, patches, and questions

- No contract change or count/rank deviation. The lead authorized the two derived raw CSVs for this task; only DESC needed a content change. After validating the 54/427/32 extraction in memory, the reviewed DESC CSV was installed with `apply_patch` and the UTF-8 readback matched the validated output exactly.
- For ambiguous mixed work, `project_type="other"` remains the honest contract fallback. When the description contains multiple distinct mileage values, miles remains null. Printed totals are never silently replaced by sums.
- No `For <lane>` patch or open question.

## Lesson

2026-09-26 — A plan's printed total can disagree with its budget columns; preserve both pieces of evidence and flag the mismatch instead of silently recalculating the total.
