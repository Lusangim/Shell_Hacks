## Deviations and open questions

- `meta.build_time` and unknown DESC source date remain null for repeatable, honest artifacts. Source URLs match `gridlock-data/README.md`. The per-row disposition ledger moved from the old uncontracted Meta fields into `source_rows.json`; `meta.stage_counts` remains integer-only and includes drop reason counts.
- Project `project_type="other"`, `voltage_kv=[]`, and `miles=null` are interim unclassified values; T1.1 must derive and verify classifications. Generic Southern Company projects relabelled Georgia Power carry `utility_basis="inferred_from_location"`. SERTP status is null because no printed status is present.
- `touch_reason="proximity"` and the measured-geometry detail do not assert a shared asset; T2.1 must refine the reason. Every overlap has `savings.status="no_cost"` because each cross-utility pair includes at least one SERTP project without a stated cost; T2.4 must compute savings where verified costs permit.
- Open question for the lead: the frozen Project contract has no `unknown` project type. Is interim `other` acceptable until T1.1, or should the contract change at G1a? No source value was invented for voltage or miles.
