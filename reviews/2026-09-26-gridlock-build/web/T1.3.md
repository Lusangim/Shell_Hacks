## 2026-09-26 focus audit correction

The ranked list remains the keyboard route to every overlap.

After the change, the Tab walk passed at 1440 and 390 (`2 passed in 2.69s`, then `2 passed in 2.81s`). `pytest tests/e2e/test_shell.py tests/e2e/audits/test_default.py -q` returned `14 passed in 26.44s`. The lead owns the broader T1.4a audit; it was not yet committed on this lane branch at this check.

2026-09-26 lesson: An SVG path can enter Chrome's Tab order without a `tabindex` attribute; test the actual Tab sequence, not just attributes.
